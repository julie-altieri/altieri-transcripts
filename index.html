About Altieri Transcripts

I am Julie Altieri, an Authorized Court Transcriptionist serving law firms, legal professionals, courts, and individuals across Ontario. I prepare certified transcripts of Ontario court proceedings with careful attention to accuracy, confidentiality, and deadlines.

I accept direct transcript orders and also work collaboratively with other authorized court transcriptionists and established transcript providers when additional capacity is required for larger matters.

My goal is to make the ordering process straightforward, with clear communication, transparent pricing, and dependable turnaround.

I follow the standards set by the Ministry of the Attorney General to support accuracy, confidentiality, and timely delivery of certified transcripts.

View my registration at Authorized Court Transcriptionist Registry .

Services & Rates

All Authorized Court Transcriptionists are mandated to charge the following rates for the production of transcripts, as per Ontario Regulation 145/22, amending Ontario Regulation 94/14 under the Administration of Justice Act. The court transcript fee schedule applies to transcript orders placed on or after April 1, 2022.

FAQ: Court transcription in Ontario

Answers from Julie Altieri, Authorized Court Transcriptionist in Ontario.

Who is the transcriptionist?

I am Julie Altieri. I am an authorized court transcriptionist in Ontario. I produce certified court transcripts for law firms, courts, and the public.

What services do you provide?

Certified Ontario court transcripts for criminal, family, civil, small claims, and POA matters. Standard, expedited, and daily turnaround are available.

How do I order a transcript?

You have three options. Use the official order form, use the Contact | Order form, or complete the prepared checklist in WhatsApp. I will use the information provided to prepare the official form and will reply with a quote and next steps.

What information do you need to start?

Case name, court file number if available, courthouse, courtroom or judge if known, court date or range of dates, and the portion of the proceeding needed.

What are the rates in Ontario?

Rates follow Ontario Regulation 145/22. Standard is $6.30 per page. Expedited is $8.80 per page. Daily is $11.75 per page. HST applies.
